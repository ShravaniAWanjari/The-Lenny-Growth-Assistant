# Ship 30 for 30 Writing Skill

## Purpose
Transform factual insights and frameworks from Lenny's Podcast into a compelling, clear, and actionable online essay following the official Ship 30 for 30 writing framework.

## Core Writing Principles (Ship 30 for 30)
1. **Clear, Engaging Headline**: State the specific problem, counter-intuitive insight, or actionable solution.
2. **The 1-3-1 Hook**:
   - 1 single opening sentence that hooks the reader.
   - 3 short sentences that build tension or context.
   - 1 impactful takeaway sentence that delivers the core thesis.
3. **Format for Skimmability**:
   - Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences maximum).
   - Use bold text for key insights and punchy phrases.
   - Use bullet points and numbered takeaways.
   - Clear section subheadings (H2 / H3).
4. **Target Length**:
   - Default target is approximately ~1,250 words (unless the user explicitly requests a shorter or specific length).
5. **Grounded Substance**:
   - Every main point must be grounded in the retrieved Lenny's Podcast transcript evidence.
   - Explicitly cite the guests, their companies, and their real-world examples (e.g. Eric Ries, Andy Johns, Naomi Gleit).
   - Never invent or fabricate quotes or metrics to fill space.
